🚀 AI Bookmark Organizer

Supercharge your Firefox bookmarks with the power of AI! 🧠 Effortlessly group and rename your saved pages based on real content, not just titles.

✨ How to Get Started

  1. 🔑 Enter your OpenAI API key
  2. 📚 Click "Organize Bookmarks" — and let the AI do the magic!

🛠️ Details You Should Know

  • Only bookmarks from the Other Bookmarks folder are organized.
  • New smart clusters are created under Organized Bookmarks.
  • Broken links? They're moved automatically to Organized Bookmarks/Unreachable.
